How to play

  • Match car brands to every row and column clue.
  • Each cell must satisfy both its row and column category.
  • Tap a row or column label for a hint about that clue.
  • Wrong guesses cost 20 points.

Show the answers?

Unsolved tiles get a magnifying glass — tap to see every brand that could fit. Your share grid still shows empty squares (⬛) for those cells.
